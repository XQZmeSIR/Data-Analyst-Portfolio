# Data Analyst Portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio! I am a Data Analyst with over 1.5 years of professional experience, specializing in turning complex, scattered data into actionable business insights. My background spans building end-to-end ETL pipelines, developing BI dashboards, and implementing data-driven solutions in both offline and online education sectors.
